# The CARE Guidelines Checklist

> **The Narrative:** A case report tells a story in a narrative format that includes the presenting concerns, clinical findings, diagnoses, interventions, outcomes (including adverse events), and follow-up. The narrative should include a discussion of the rationale for any conclusions and any take-away messages.

## Detailed Checklist Format

### 1. Title
- [ ] Includes the words **"case report"** (or **"case study"**).
- [ ] States the phenomenon of greatest interest (e.g., symptom, diagnosis, test, intervention).

### 2. Keywords
- [ ] 2 to 5 keywords identifying the key elements of this case.

### 3. Abstract
- [ ] **3a. Introduction:** What does this case add?
- [ ] **3b. Case Presentation:**
  - [ ] Main symptoms of the patient
  - [ ] Main clinical findings
  - [ ] Main diagnoses and interventions
  - [ ] Main outcomes
- [ ] **3c. Conclusion:** Main "take-away" lessons from this case.

### 4. Introduction
- [ ] Brief background summary referencing relevant medical literature.

### 5. Patient Information
- [ ] **5a. Demographic details:** Age, gender, ethnicity, occupation, etc.
- [ ] **5b. Chief complaint:** Main symptoms reported by the patient.
- [ ] **5c. History:** Medical, family, and psychosocial history (diet, lifestyle, genetic info, relevant comorbidities, past interventions, and outcomes).

### 6. Clinical Findings
- [ ] Relevant physical examination (PE) findings.

### 7. Timeline
- [ ] Important dates and milestones depicted in a chronological table or figure.

### 8. Diagnostic Assessment
- [ ] **8a. Diagnostic methods:** PE, lab tests, imaging, questionnaires.
- [ ] **8b. Diagnostic challenges:** Financial, cultural, or language barriers.
- [ ] **8c. Diagnostic reasoning:** Differential diagnoses considered.
- [ ] **8d. Prognostic characteristics:** Staging or risk assessment where applicable.

### 9. Therapeutic Intervention
- [ ] **9a. Intervention details:** Type (pharmacologic, surgical, preventive, self-care).
- [ ] Administration parameters: Dosage, strength, duration.
- [ ] Modifications / changes in intervention along with rationale.

### 10. Follow-up and Outcomes
- [ ] Summary of clinical course across follow-up visits.
- [ ] Clinician- and patient-assessed outcomes.
- [ ] Key follow-up test results (positive or negative).
- [ ] Treatment adherence, tolerability, and assessment methods.
- [ ] Adverse and unanticipated events.

### 11. Discussion
- [ ] **11a.** Strengths and limitations of case management.
- [ ] **11b.** Context within the relevant medical literature.
- [ ] **11c.** Rationale for conclusions and cause-and-effect assessment.
- [ ] **11d.** Main take-away lessons.

### 12. Patient Perspective
- [ ] Patient's personal perspective or experience shared (where feasible).

### 13. Informed Consent
- [ ] Confirmation that the patient provided informed consent (available upon request).
