# Minimal Checklist for Manuscripts Not Covered by the Seven Design-Specific Guidelines

This checklist applies when a manuscript's study design does not match any of the seven reporting guidelines held in this reference set (ARRIVE, CARE, CONSORT, COREQ, PRISMA, STARD, TREND). Typical cases: narrative (non-systematic) reviews, data descriptor articles, study protocols, and other article types PMC recognizes as primary content (see `application_guideline.md`).

It is derived from the article-type expectations in `application_guideline.md` ("Article content" and the review/case-report/protocol paragraph) and from `synthesis.md` section 2.5 ("Requirements specific to article types").

## 1. Narrative (non-systematic) review

| No. | Item | What to verify |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| R1 | Scope and currency | Does the review comprehensively cover recent research in the field, rather than being dominated by the authors' own prior work? |
| R2 | Search/selection transparency | Is there at least a brief description of how the literature discussed was identified and selected? |
| R3 | Critical analysis | Does the review critically analyze and synthesize the literature rather than merely summarizing it article by article? |
| R4 | Balance | Are conflicting findings or schools of thought in the field acknowledged and discussed? |
| R5 | Conclusions in scope | Do the conclusions stay within what the reviewed literature supports? |

## 2. Data descriptor article

| No. | Item | What to verify |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| D1 | Dataset location/access | Is the location and access method for the dataset (repository, accession number, URL) clearly stated? |
| D2 | Collection methodology | Is the data collection method described in enough detail to be reproducible? |
| D3 | Quality control | Are quality-control or validation procedures for the data described? |
| D4 | Reuse conditions | Are licensing terms and any restrictions on data reuse stated? |
| D5 | Documentation | Is the data dictionary/variable documentation sufficient for a third party to use the dataset correctly? |

## 3. Study protocol

| No. | Item | What to verify |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| P1 | Sample size justification | Is the rationale/calculation for the planned sample size provided? |
| P2 | Materials and procedures | Are materials, equipment, and procedures described completely enough for another team to replicate the planned study? |
| P3 | Analysis plan | Is the planned statistical/data analysis approach specified in advance? |
| P4 | Ethical requirements | Are the ethics approval status (or plan to obtain it) and consent procedures described? |
| P5 | Registration | Is the protocol registered (e.g., trial registry, PROSPERO) where applicable, with the registration number stated? |

## 4. Common minimum items (apply regardless of article type)

| No. | Item | What to verify |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| C1 | Abstract completeness | Does the abstract, on its own, accurately and completely describe the article's content, consistent with ICMJE recommendations? |
| C2 | Aim-conclusion alignment | Do the stated aims and the conclusions match, without unsupported generalization? |
| C3 | Citation adequacy | Does every substantive claim carry a citation, and does the citation actually support the claim? |
| C4 | Table/figure non-duplication | Is data presented once, without redundant repetition between text and tables/figures? |
| C5 | Editorial quality | Is the writing clear, grammatically correct, and free of undefined or single-use abbreviations? |

Use this checklist together with Checklist A (submission stage), Checklist B (review stage), and Checklist C (editorial stage) from `synthesis.md`, which remain fully applicable regardless of article type.
